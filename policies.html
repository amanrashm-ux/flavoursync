Order Policies

Clear notes before the kitchen starts cooking.

Order Confirmation

Orders are confirmed on WhatsApp after checking current availability, delivery locality, timing and final bill.

Scheduled Orders

Scheduled requests are accepted after kitchen confirmation. Larger orders may need extra preparation time.

Delivery Area

Delivery support depends on exact locality, distance, order size and available delivery partners.

Cancellation

Cancellation is easiest before preparation begins. Once cooking starts, cancellation may not be possible for made-to-order items.

Allergy Notes

Customers should mention allergies or dietary restrictions clearly on WhatsApp before confirmation.

Privacy

Contact details shared through forms are used only to prepare the WhatsApp message on the customer's device.
